# Kingdom Hearts Canon List

This document tracks every major Kingdom Hearts title, platform release, system/port, and non-game media in canonical order by internal timeline. Use it as the master lore reference for `Kingdom Hearts 0: Door to Darkness` and the wider `kingdom-hearts-zero` project.

---

## Canonical Lore Timeline (In-Universe)

### 1. Age of Fairy Tales / Era of Peace
- **χ era / Unchained χ / Union χ** — Foretellers, Dandelions, Union leaders, Dark Road setup.
- **Kingdom Hearts χ Back Cover** — Film retelling the χ-era conflict from the Foretellers' perspective.

### 2. Keyblade War and Fragmentation
- **Dark Road** — Xehanort's youth, Eraqus, Scala ad Caelum, seeds of the war.
- **Kingdom Hearts χ** — Keyblade War, χ-blade shattered, hearts scattered.
- **Kingdom Hearts χ Unchained χ / Union χ** — Post-war simulation and rebuild framing.
- **Kingdom Hearts χ Back Cover** — Foretellers’ roles during the war.

### 3. Modern Era Begins
- **Kingdom Hearts 0 Birth By Sleep** — Terra, Aqua, Ventus; Master Xehanort plan; Vanitas, Unversed; fate of the Destiny Islands and Destiny.
- **Kingdom Hearts 0 Birth By Sleep 0.2 A Fragmentary Passage** — Aqua's fall into the Realm of Darkness.

### 4. Kingdom Hearts 1 Era
- **Kingdom Hearts** — Sora, Riku, Kairi; Destiny Islands destroyed; Ansem the Wise/Seeker of Darkness; Hollow Bastion; worlds restored.
- **Kingdom Hearts: Chain of Memories** — Castle Oblivion; Organization XIII introduced; Namine; Roxas born.
- **Kingdom Hearts 358/2 Days** — Roxas in Organization XIII; Xion; Days 1–358; fake Twilight Town.

### 5. Kingdom Hearts 2 Era
- **Kingdom Hearts 2** — Sora returns; Twilight Town prologue; Organization XIII downfall; Xemnas; Roxas merges; Kingdom Hearts formed/released.
- **Kingdom Hearts 2 Final Mix** — Expanded cutscenes and fights.

### 6. Birth By Sleep / 0.2 Expanded Context
- **Kingdom Hearts 0 Birth By Sleep 0.2 A Fragmentary Passage** — Direct lead-in to KH1 from Aqua’s perspective.
- **Kingdom Hearts χ Back Cover** — The Foretellers and Master of Masters.

### 7. Coded / Re:coded / Days Context
- **Kingdom Hearts Re:coded** — Jiminy Cricket data-world investigation; Sora-as-data.
- **Kingdom Hearts 358/2 Days** — Roxas/Xion/Organization XIII days.

### 8. Dream Drop Distance Era
- **Kingdom Hearts 3D Dream Drop Distance** — Sora and Riku Mark of Mastery exam; Sleeping Worlds; Xehanort return plan finalized; seven guardian of light requirement; χ-blade recreation plan.

### 9. Kingdom Hearts 3 Era
- **Kingdom Hearts 3** — Sora, Donald, Goofy; Realm of Darkness; Keyblade Graveyard; Master Xehanort; χ-blade; end of Xehanort saga; Final Battle; post-game Re Mind DLC.
- **Kingdom Hearts 3 Re Mind** — Scala ad Caelum; Yozora; lingering finale threads.
- **Kingdom Hearts III + Re Mind Final Mix** — Definitive version.

### 10. Melody of Memory
- **Kingdom Hearts Melody of Memory** — Rhythm game; post-KH3; Kairi; Remnant dream data; hints toward the next arc.

### 11. Kingdom Hearts 4 / Lost Master Arc (Forthcoming)
- **Kingdom Hearts 4** — Announced; set in Quadratum/Shinjoku; new world; new protagonist; post-KH3/Re Mind.

## Key Canon Concepts for KH0 Design

- χ-blade: forged from light + darkness in equal measure; shattered in Keyblade War.
- Keyblades: originally chose their wielders; now forged/passed; pass through many hands.
- Keyblade Inheritance Ceremony: transfers ability and power; essential lore for successors.
- Nobody: body and heart separated; creature of emptiness; retains appearance, loses heart.
- Unversed: vanitas fragments born from negativity; pre-/during BBS.
- Dream Eater: Spirit/ nightmare entities in DDD.
- Realm of Darkness: where hearts of pure darkness fall; heartless origin realm.
- Keyblade Graveyard: final battlefield; locus of χ-blade recreation.
- Master Xehanort plan: thirteen darknesses in thirteen darknesses; seven guardians of light.
- Foretellers: Ira, Invi, Aced, Ava, Gula; Master of Masters; Book of Prophecy.
- Luxu: ancient keyblade wielder; passes keyblade through ages.
